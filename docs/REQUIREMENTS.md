# Requirements

The authoritative role matrix, workflows, grading rules, and confirmed decisions are in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md sections 1, 4 and Basis. Avoid duplicating them here.

Glossary:
- Subject: persistent catalog entry.
- Offering: one teacher teaching one subject in one term, potentially across several sections.
- Section: a term-bound student group.
- Enrollment: a student's effective offering association, including regular roster derivation or an explicit irregular exception.
- Draft: faculty working content, private until explicit publication.
- Assessment result: a released individual score/feedback snapshot.
- Overall grade: separately published Midterm, Finals or Course calculation.
- Progress: student-reported completion and actual submissions; separate from faculty coverage and grades.

Business process documentation follows the accepted setup -> enrollment -> teaching publication -> student assessment -> faculty grading/release -> grade publication -> closure/reopening flow.
